The Rug Size Guide

5 min read

The right rug does more than fit a room. It defines its proportions, anchors the furniture and creates a sense of balance.

While colour, texture and pattern naturally attract our attention, proportion is often what determines whether an interior feels balanced or unfinished. A rug that is too small can make even the most beautifully designed room feel disconnected, while one with the right proportions creates harmony, anchors the furniture and allows the space to breathe.

Choosing the right Moroccan rug is not simply about finding a size that fits the room. It is about understanding how the rug interacts with the architecture, the furniture and the way the space is lived in.

Thoughtful proportions rarely draw attention to themselves.

They simply make everything else feel right.

Why Rug Size Matters

A rug does far more than cover the floor.

It defines spaces within an interior, introduces warmth and texture, and creates visual balance between furniture and architecture.

The right size helps connect the different elements of a room into a cohesive whole.

When a rug is too small, furniture appears to float independently, making the room feel fragmented. When the proportions are generous, the furniture feels grounded and the entire space becomes more inviting.

For this reason, choosing the right rug size is often more important than choosing the right colour or pattern.

Living Room

The living room is where proportion has the greatest impact.

As a general principle, larger rugs create a greater sense of cohesion and openness.

Ideally, the front legs of your sofa and armchairs should rest on the rug. In larger rooms, placing all furniture completely on the rug creates an even stronger feeling of balance.

Very small rugs positioned only beneath a coffee table often interrupt the flow of the room rather than defining it.

If you are deciding between two sizes, the larger option is usually the better choice.

A generously proportioned Moroccan rug allows the seating area to feel intentional and visually connected.

Dining Room

A dining rug should comfortably accommodate both the table and every chair, even when the chairs are pulled out.

This ensures movement feels natural while preserving balanced proportions throughout the room.

As a general guideline, the rug should extend well beyond the edges of the dining table on every side.

This creates a more generous composition while preventing chairs from catching on the edge of the rug during everyday use.

Whether your table is round, rectangular or oval, generous proportions almost always produce the most elegant result.

Bedroom

A Moroccan rug introduces softness, warmth and comfort to the bedroom.

The ideal size depends on the room layout and the desired effect.

A large rug positioned beneath the bed creates a calm, unified composition while providing softness underfoot when getting up each morning.

Alternatively, smaller rugs can be placed on either side of the bed or at its foot to introduce texture while maintaining a lighter visual presence.

There is no single correct approach.

The best choice depends on the scale of the room and the atmosphere you wish to create.

Hallways

Hallways are often overlooked, yet they provide one of the strongest opportunities to introduce warmth and character into a home.

A hallway runner naturally guides movement while softening long architectural lines.

Rather than filling the entire width of the corridor, leave a consistent border of flooring visible on each side.

This simple detail creates a more refined appearance and allows the runner to feel intentionally placed rather than wall-to-wall.

Entryways

The entrance to a home creates the first impression.

A thoughtfully chosen Moroccan rug introduces warmth from the moment you walk through the door.

Depending on the size of the space, an entryway rug may simply define the entrance or become a visual transition into the rest of the home.

Maintaining comfortable circulation around the rug helps preserve a sense of openness while allowing the architecture to remain visible.

Balance Before Measurement

Although standard rug dimensions provide useful guidance, every home is different.

Furniture layouts, architectural details, and circulation patterns all influence what feels visually balanced.

Rather than choosing a rug solely because it matches standard measurements, consider how the rug relates to the room as a whole.

Sometimes increasing the dimensions by only a small amount transforms the entire space.

A well-proportioned rug rarely draws attention to its size.

It simply allows the room to feel complete.

One Rug, Many Homes

A timeless Moroccan rug is never defined by a single room.

As homes evolve, furniture changes and interiors are reimagined, a well-made rug continues to adapt naturally.

The same rug may begin its life in a living room before finding its place in a bedroom, a reading space, or another home entirely.

Its proportions remain generous.

Its craftsmanship remains timeless.

Its beauty continues to evolve.

This versatility is one of the defining qualities of a thoughtfully designed handmade rug.

Choosing a Bespoke Rug

Sometimes the perfect size simply doesn't exist.

Large open-plan interiors, unusually proportioned rooms or specific furniture layouts often call for dimensions beyond standard sizes.

This is where a bespoke Moroccan rug offers complete freedom.

Rather than adapting your home to fit an existing rug, a bespoke piece allows you to choose the dimensions that feel most balanced for your space while maintaining the timeless proportions that define great interior design.

The result is a rug that feels effortless today while remaining versatile enough to move with you as your home evolves.

Timeless Proportions Never Go Out of Style

Interior trends constantly change.

Good proportions do not.

Long after colours, furniture and decorative styles have evolved, a well-proportioned rug continues to create balance and harmony within a room.

This is why choosing the right size is one of the most important decisions you can make.

A thoughtfully sized Moroccan rug becomes more than a decorative element.

It becomes the quiet foundation upon which the rest of the interior is built.
