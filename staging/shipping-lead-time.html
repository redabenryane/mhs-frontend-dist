Shipping &
Lead Time

Made entirely by hand in Morocco · Worldwide delivery

Production

Production

Each made-to-order rug is crafted by hand. Production time varies depending on dimensions and design complexity, and an estimated timeline is confirmed with your order.

  • Rugs up to 9 × 12 ft: approximately 12–16 weeks
  • Larger or more complex pieces: approximately 16–20 weeks
Vintage Rugs

Vintage Rugs

Vintage rugs are one-of-a-kind and available without production lead time. An estimated dispatch date will be confirmed after your order is placed.

Worldwide Delivery

Worldwide Delivery

We ship worldwide through trusted logistics partners. Shipping costs and estimated delivery times vary according to destination and rug dimensions and are confirmed with your order.

Custom, Duties & Taxes

Custom, Duties & Taxes

International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes or local charges imposed by the destination country. Unless stated otherwise, these charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in the order or shipping price.

Tracking

Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive a shipping confirmation with tracking details.

Handmade Characteristics

Handmade Characteristics

Final dimensions may vary slightly. Subtle variations in tone, texture, pattern and finishing are inherent to the handmade process and are part of the character of each rug.
